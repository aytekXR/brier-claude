6.2 · Constraints & rules

Hard constraints & do / don’t

Non-negotiable constraints
  • Warm paper background #F4F2EC, never stark white or dark-mode-by-default.
  • Saturated color only for FAS bands & status — everything else is ink/paper/navy.
  • Every score sits beside its evidence (clip + timestamp + chart).
  • Source Serif 4 headlines · IBM Plex Sans UI · IBM Plex Mono for all data.
  • No buy/sell/hold language anywhere. The product reports; it never advises.
Tone the design must carry
premiumintelligentanalyticaltrustworthyhigh-performancedata-drivensophisticatedmodern SaaSbuilt for serious traders

If a generated screen would look at home on a meme “exposed” account or a generic crypto landing page, it is wrong — regenerate toward the rating-agency reference set.

Always

  • Use hairlines and whitespace for structure; tabular figures for all numbers.
  • Make the leaderboard/receipt the hero — data is the imagery.
  • Keep the register-mark dot as the single point of Brier Blue.
  • Write neutral, sourced, non-pejorative copy.

Never

  • Gradient/neon/glassmorphism, glows, 3-D charts, rockets, flames, emoji.
  • Default fonts (Inter/Roboto), stock-photo “traders,” or invented stats.
  • Recommendation language, callout/dunk tone, or fake testimonials.
  • Heavy drop-shadows or rounded “card-with-left-accent-border” tropes.
6.3 · The portable brief

Master prompt block

Paste verbatim. Tuned to be tool-agnostic — it describes outcomes and tokens, not implementation.

# BRIER — MASTER DESIGN BRIEF PRODUCT Brier — the rating agency for public prediction. An independent engine that extracts falsifiable predictions from crypto YouTube analysts, resolves them against price, and publishes a base-rate-corrected accuracy score (FAS 0–100) with a clip-level receipt behind every claim. It reports who has been right; it NEVER recommends what to buy. FEELING Premium, intelligent, analytical, trustworthy, data-driven, sophisticated, modern SaaS for serious traders. A referee's scorecard, not an opinion. "Boring on purpose." REFERENCES Moody's/FICO methodology docs · Michelin authority · Bloomberg Terminal density · Economist/FT broadsheet · TradingView charts. NOT crypto-startup neon, NOT a meme "exposed" account. COLOR Background warm paper #F4F2EC. Ink #16191D. Institutional navy #16233A. One accent — Brier Blue #2D54CE — for links/focus/the mark, used sparingly. FAS band ramp (the only saturated scale): anti-skilled #B06043 · coin-flip #A8995F · skilled #3E7A78 · elite #0E7C66. Status: hit #0E7C66, miss #B23A2E, partial #BC8A2C, open #4A6CC7, void #8A8F96. TYPE Headlines + wordmark = Source Serif 4 (600). UI + body = IBM Plex Sans. ALL numbers/tickers/timestamps/scores = IBM Plex Mono, tabular. FORM 3px radius, flat cards, hairline rules, generous whitespace. The leaderboard table IS the hero. FAS badge in band color. Status chips muted + bordered. Trend sparklines. Quiet top nav, no app sidebar on the public site. Mobile-first; tables collapse to cards under 640px. WCAG AA. LOGO A registration/crosshair mark inside a rounded square frame, with a single Brier-Blue dot at the crosshair center = "one prediction located on the record." Monochrome except the dot. MOTION 120–200ms ease-out, small moves. Numbers count up to rest, never bounce. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. VOICE Plain, exact, neutral, sourced. Cite numbers, don't characterize. NEVER: buy/sell/hold, hype, dunk/"rekt/exposed", emoji, gradients, neon, glassmorphism, 3-D charts, stock-photo traders, invented stats, Inter/Roboto. TASK Design [SCREEN/ASSET] for Brier honoring every rule above.
