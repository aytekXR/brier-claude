Visual storytelling rules

Do

  • Lead with a real resolved claim and its receipt.
  • Let the numbers and the chart be the hero image.
  • Keep the same voice in ads as in the product: plain, sourced, neutral.

Don’t

  • Imply “follow this person” or “buy this” — ever.
  • Dunk on a named analyst or use “rekt / exposed” language.
  • Invent stats, fake testimonials, or stock-photo “traders.”
