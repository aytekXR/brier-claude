How to read this book

One source of truth, end to end

Every decision in this document is derived from the two source artifacts: the Prediction Track-Record Engine PRD (v1.1) and the Lean Canvas / Value-Proposition Canvas (v2.0). Nothing here is generic startup branding — the strategy, palette, type, and UI all express one positioning: a neutral referee that proves who has been right, with receipts.

Scope note — product reframe The working brief framed Brier as a personal “trading journal” with trade tagging and psychological tracking. The source documents describe a different product: an independent accuracy-rating engine that scores public crypto YouTube analysts. Brier never scores the reader’s own trades and never recommends instruments. Where the brief’s template asked for “journal / trade-tagging / psych-tracking” workflows, this book maps them to Brier’s real workflows — leaderboard, analyst pages, clip-level receipts, the QA review queue, disputes, methodology, and creator badges.
Phase 01 · Deep Understanding

Strategic summary

Retail investors move real money on the say-so of analysts with no verifiable record. The market rewards confidence over skill. Brier resolves that asymmetry by treating public predictions as falsifiable claims — extracting them, resolving them against price, and scoring each analyst with statistical honesty and a clip you can replay.

“We don’t tell you what to buy. We tell you who has been right — with receipts.” Brier · Unique value proposition

The strategic core

MissionWhy we exist
Make public prediction accountable. Replace follower counts and confidence with a resolved, receipt-backed record of who was actually right.
VisionWhere it goes
Become the credit-rating agency for forecasts — the credential a public analyst displays to prove a track record instead of claiming one, across crypto first and every prediction domain next.
Unique valueThe wedge
Independent, base-rate-corrected, calibration-aware accuracy scores for public analysts, with a clip-level receipt — player, timestamp, and price chart — behind every claim. We sell the scoreboard, never the trade.
CategoryReference set
Rating agency, not callout account. The FICO / Nielsen / Michelin / Moody’s archetype: clinical, neutral, versioned, boring on purpose. The legal posture depends on never looking like a meme “exposed” account.
Brand promiseWhat we guarantee
Every score links to a clip, a timestamp, and a chart. The methodology is public and versioned. The product never says buy or sell.

Pains we solve & outcomes we create

Pain → resolved
  • Can’t tell skill from confidence before risking money.
  • No way to settle “is X legit” beyond gut feel and Reddit.
  • Honest creators can’t prove they differ from the noise.
  • Journalists lack a defensible source on accuracy.
Functional + emotional outcome
  • Functional: filter 10–30 noisy sources to the few with real skill; verify a record in one click.
  • Emotional: vindication and clarity — “the one who wasn’t fooled.” Checking Brier feels like reading a referee’s scorecard, not another opinion.

The product narrative

A confident voice on YouTube tells a million people what comes next. Most are wrong; the loudest attract the largest followings. Brier listens to the public record, isolates the falsifiable claims, waits for the market to resolve them, and keeps an append-only ledger of who was right — corrected for how easy each call was, and how well-calibrated the caller is. The output is one number per analyst, the FAS, and behind it a wall of receipts. No advice. No callouts. Just the scorecard that ends the argument.
