Phase 02 · Brand Strategy

Brand foundation

The fixed points the whole brand rotates around. If a decision contradicts this page, the decision is wrong — not the page.

2.1 · The foundation
MissionWhy we exist

Make public prediction accountable. Turn the firehose of confident calls into a resolved, receipt-backed record so audiences can tell skill from noise before they risk money.

VisionThe 10-year picture

To be the rating agency for forecasts — the independent standard a public analyst displays to prove a track record instead of claiming one. Crypto first, then equities, then every domain where someone predicts in public.

Brand promiseWhat we guarantee

Every score links to a clip, a timestamp, and a price chart. The methodology is public and versioned. Disputes get a 7-day answer. And we never tell you what to buy.

Brand essenceTwo words

Proof, not opinion.

2.2 · Brand values

Six commitments

Values are not adjectives on a wall — each one is enforced somewhere in the product or the legal posture. That is what makes them credible.

VAL-01

Receipts over rhetoric

No claim publishes without a clip, an offset, and a chart. If we can’t show it, we don’t score it.

VAL-02

Statistical honesty

Base-rate correction, calibration, Bayesian shrinkage, a minimum n. We score skill, not luck or volume.

VAL-03

Neutral, never pejorative

A referee, not a callout account. Reviewed style guide enforces non-pejorative language site-wide.

VAL-04

Only the falsifiable

If a statement can’t be proven right or wrong against price, it doesn’t enter the score — it counts toward the falsifiability ratio instead.

VAL-05

Show the work

Public versioned methodology, append-only ledger, public corrections log. No silent edits, ever.

VAL-06

Unbuyable independence

The scored can never influence their own score. No pay-to-improve. No advice products. A hard regulatory firewall.

2.3 · Personality & voice

Five personality traits

Brier behaves like a courtroom expert witness: precise, unhurried, and impossible to rattle because the evidence is already on the table.

01 — ClinicalPrecise, unemotional
Exact numbers, defined terms, no adjectives doing an argument’s job. The tone of a methodology paper, not a hot take.
02 — RigorousEvidence-bound
Every assertion is backed and reproducible. We would rather publish a smaller, defensible claim than a louder, shakier one.
03 — NeutralEven-handed referee
We don’t cheer, dunk, or editorialize. A high score and a low score are written in exactly the same register.
04 — TransparentShows its working
Formulas, base rates, versions, and corrections are all public. Trust is earned by being inspectable, not by being trusted.
05 — Quietly confidentBoring on purpose
We don’t need to raise our voice. The receipts are louder than any claim we could make about them.

Tone of voice

Voice principles
  • Declarative and plain. Short sentences. Tabular numbers.
  • Cite, don’t characterize: “HIT on 14 Jul at $80,140,” not “nailed it.”
  • Never advise, hype, or imply action. No “buy / sell / hold,” ever.
  • Name the method and the version when stating a score.
  • Concede uncertainty openly — “provisional,” “n below threshold,” “disputed.”
Communication style
  • Register: wire service meets ratings methodology.
  • Person: institutional “we”; the analyst is named, never insulted.
  • Numbers: always sourced, always units, always tabular.
  • Humor: dry at most, and never at a scored person’s expense.

Voice in practice

Messaging pillars

MSG-01

Receipts, not opinions

A clip, a timestamp, a chart behind every claim.

MSG-02

Skill, minus the luck

Base-rate-corrected and calibration-aware. We score the caller, not the coin flip.

MSG-03

The credential that proves it

Brier Verified — the only badge that proves a record instead of claiming one.

MSG-04

The scoreboard, never the trade

We tell you who was right. We never tell you what to do.

2.4 · Brand archetypes

The Referee, backed by the Ruler

Brier’s authority is borrowed from two archetypes working together: the impartial judge who only cares what the evidence says, and the institution that sets the standard everyone else is measured against.

Primary archetype

The Sage / Referee

Driven by truth and objectivity. The Sage seeks what is real and reports it without flinching; the Referee applies a known rulebook identically to everyone. This is why Brier reads as neutral and inspectable rather than as an activist account.

Expresses as

methodologyevidenceimpartialityrulebookverification
Secondary archetype

The Ruler / Standard-setter

The institution that defines the measure — FICO, Moody’s, Nielsen, Michelin. It confers status by certifying. This is why “Brier Verified” can become a credential creators want to display and brands trust to vet by.

Expresses as

the standardcertificationauthoritycredentialorder
What we are deliberately NOT Brier is not the Outlaw or the Jester — not a meme “exposed / rekt” callout account. That posture would be louder and cheaper to grow, but it is legally fragile and strategically off-brand. The category note in the source is explicit: “clinical, neutral, versioned, boring on purpose; not a callout account.”
